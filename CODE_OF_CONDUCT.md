# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

DMDE Master Handbook contributors commit to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for everyone involved in the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus discussions on the work, not individuals.
- Welcome different levels of experience and diverse perspectives.
- Assume good intent while still addressing issues clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring documented contribution or review guidance.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate discussions, request changes in behavior, close conversations, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct. Concerns should be raised privately with project maintainers whenever possible.
