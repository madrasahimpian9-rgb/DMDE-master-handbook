# DMDE-00.04 Objectives

## Objective Categories

The DMDE Master Handbook objectives are grouped into strategic, product, delivery, operational, and governance outcomes.

## Strategic Objectives

- Establish a shared documentation foundation for the DMDE platform.
- Improve alignment between sponsors, product owners, architects, delivery teams, and maintainers.
- Reduce ambiguity in business domains, platform responsibilities, and architectural direction.

## Product Objectives

- Document platform capabilities and domain boundaries in a way that supports prioritization.
- Capture functional and non-functional requirements with enough clarity for review and implementation.
- Provide traceability from vision and scope to roadmap, requirements, architecture, and success criteria.

## Delivery Objectives

- Standardize documentation structure and naming across all handbook areas.
- Provide development guidance that supports consistent implementation and review.
- Enable contributors to update documentation as part of normal delivery work.

## Operational Objectives

- Document expectations for security, deployment, logging, error handling, testing, and release management.
- Support maintainable operations through architecture, monitoring, and governance references.
- Reduce operational risk by keeping critical decisions and standards visible.

## Governance Objectives

| Objective | Expected Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Clear ownership | Stakeholder and governance documents identify accountable roles. |
| Decision traceability | Architecture and decision logs record material choices and rationale. |
| Controlled change | Scope, roadmap, and changelog reflect approved changes. |
| Documentation quality | Contributors follow style, structure, and review expectations. |
