# DMDE-00.05 Stakeholders

## Stakeholder Overview

DMDE stakeholders include the people and groups who sponsor, govern, define, deliver, operate, use, or are affected by the platform and its documentation.

## Stakeholder Groups

| Group | Interest | Typical Involvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsors | Strategic value, investment alignment, governance confidence. | Approve direction, priorities, and major scope changes. |
| Product owners | Business outcomes, requirements, adoption, and acceptance. | Define priorities, validate requirements, and confirm acceptance criteria. |
| Business stakeholders | Process fit, reporting accuracy, usability, and policy alignment. | Review workflows, rules, personas, and business requirements. |
| Architecture stakeholders | Platform coherence, standards, integration, security, and scalability. | Review architecture decisions, domain boundaries, and standards. |
| Delivery teams | Clear requirements, implementation guidance, and maintainable workflow. | Build capabilities and update documentation impacted by changes. |
| Operations and support | Reliability, monitoring, incident response, release readiness. | Validate operational guidance and support procedures. |
| End users | Usable services, consistent data, and reliable access. | Provide feedback through discovery, validation, and adoption channels. |

## RACI Snapshot

| Activity | Sponsor | Product owner | Architect | Delivery team | Maintainer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approve charter | A | R | C | I | C |
| Define requirements | C | A/R | C | C | I |
| Approve architecture decisions | C | C | A/R | C | I |
| Update documentation | I | C | C | R | A/R |
| Validate release readiness | C | A | C | R | C |

## Communication Cadence

- Review charter and roadmap updates during major planning cycles.
- Review requirements and domain changes during discovery and delivery planning.
- Review architecture decisions when a change affects standards, integrations, security, or operations.
- Review documentation quality before merging material handbook updates.
