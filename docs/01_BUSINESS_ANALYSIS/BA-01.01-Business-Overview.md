# BA-01.01 Business Overview

## Business Context

DMDE supports digital education operations by organizing academic, administrative, reporting, portal, and supporting service capabilities into a coherent platform. The handbook helps stakeholders align around common definitions before implementation decisions are made.

## Value Proposition

- Reduce fragmentation across academic and operational workflows.
- Improve visibility into student, teacher, curriculum, assessment, reporting, and portal information.
- Standardize business rules and requirements across domains.
- Support reliable decision-making through consistent data and documented processes.
- Improve onboarding for contributors and stakeholders through a navigable documentation baseline.

## Business Outcomes

| Outcome | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Operational consistency | Processes and rules are documented and applied consistently across modules. |
| Data confidence | Shared definitions support trustworthy reporting and analytics. |
| Stakeholder clarity | Users, owners, and delivery teams understand responsibilities and expectations. |
| Delivery readiness | Requirements and priorities are clear enough to guide planning and implementation. |

## Operating Assumptions

- DMDE will serve multiple stakeholder groups with different access needs and workflows.
- Business processes may vary by institution, so configurable rules and clear defaults are important.
- Reporting, auditability, and security are core business expectations rather than optional technical features.
