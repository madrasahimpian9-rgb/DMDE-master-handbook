# BA-01.02 Business Capabilities

## Capability Map

Business capabilities describe what DMDE must enable, independent of a specific technology solution. They provide a stable structure for requirements, ownership, domain architecture, and roadmap planning.

| Capability Group | Capabilities | Business Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and access | Authentication, authorization, roles, permissions, account lifecycle. | Protects access and supports secure user experiences. |
| Organization management | Institution profile, units, departments, classes, groups, hierarchy. | Provides structural context for academic and administrative workflows. |
| Academic structure | Academic years, terms, levels, programs, subjects, schedules. | Establishes the operating model for curriculum and learning delivery. |
| Student management | Student records, enrollment, status changes, profiles, guardians. | Maintains accurate student lifecycle information. |
| Teacher management | Teacher profiles, assignments, availability, workload, competencies. | Supports instructional planning and staffing visibility. |
| Curriculum and assessment | Curriculum planning, competencies, assessment setup, grading rules. | Aligns learning outcomes with evaluation and progress tracking. |
| Reporting and analytics | Dashboards, exports, scorecards, operational metrics. | Enables data-informed decisions and transparency. |
| Communication and portals | Notifications, public portal, CMS, mobile/PWA access. | Improves stakeholder engagement and access to information. |
| Document management | Templates, generated documents, storage, approvals. | Standardizes records and reduces manual administrative effort. |
| AI services | Assistance, recommendations, summarization, analytics support. | Enhances productivity where governance and validation are in place. |

## Capability Prioritization Criteria

- Alignment with project objectives and stakeholder needs.
- Impact on core academic and operational workflows.
- Dependency on identity, organization, data, or integration foundations.
- Risk reduction, compliance value, and reporting value.
- Readiness of requirements and business ownership.
