# BA-01.03 Organization Structure

## Purpose

The organization structure describes the groups, roles, and decision relationships that influence DMDE requirements, governance, and operations.

## Representative Organization Model

| Layer | Description | Example Actors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance | Sets strategic direction, approves scope, and resolves escalations. | Sponsor, steering committee, executive leadership. |
| Product and business ownership | Defines business priorities, validates workflows, and accepts outcomes. | Product owner, business owner, domain lead. |
| Academic operations | Owns academic structure, curriculum, assessment, and student progress processes. | Academic director, curriculum team, assessment coordinator. |
| Administration | Maintains institution, staff, student, document, and reporting operations. | Administrator, registrar, office staff. |
| Delivery and architecture | Designs, builds, reviews, and maintains the platform and documentation. | Architect, analyst, engineer, QA, documentation maintainer. |
| Support and operations | Ensures service reliability, release readiness, monitoring, and user support. | Support lead, operations team, help desk. |

## Decision Paths

- Strategic scope decisions should be escalated to governance stakeholders.
- Process and policy decisions should be validated by product and business owners.
- Architecture decisions should be reviewed by architecture stakeholders before implementation.
- Operational decisions should include support and operations stakeholders early enough to assess readiness.

## Documentation Implications

Organization changes may affect permissions, reporting, workflow ownership, approval rules, and escalation paths. Update related requirements and business rules when organizational responsibilities change.
