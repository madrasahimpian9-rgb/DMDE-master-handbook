# BA-01.05 Business Processes

## Process Documentation Approach

Business processes describe how stakeholders accomplish outcomes across roles, data, systems, and rules. Each process should identify triggers, actors, steps, outputs, exceptions, and related requirements.

## Core Process Inventory

| Process | Trigger | Primary Actors | Expected Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User onboarding | A new user needs platform access. | Administrator, user, identity owner. | Active account with correct role and permissions. |
| Academic year setup | A new academic cycle is planned. | Academic administrator, curriculum lead. | Academic periods, levels, classes, subjects, and schedules. |
| Student enrollment | A student joins or changes status. | Registrar, student, guardian, administrator. | Current student record and enrollment status. |
| Teacher assignment | Teaching responsibilities are planned. | Academic operations, teacher, administrator. | Teacher-to-class or teacher-to-subject assignments. |
| Curriculum planning | Curriculum structure is created or revised. | Curriculum team, academic leadership. | Approved curriculum map and competency structure. |
| Assessment execution | Assessment cycle begins. | Teacher, student, assessment coordinator. | Assessment scores, feedback, and progress records. |
| Reporting review | Stakeholders need operational insight. | Leadership, analyst, administrator. | Validated dashboard, report, or export. |
| Notification delivery | Stakeholders need timely communication. | Administrator, teacher, system service. | Message sent through configured channel with delivery status. |
| Document generation | A formal record or template is requested. | Administrator, approver, requester. | Generated document with approval or audit trail. |

## Process Quality Criteria

- Steps are ordered and understandable by business stakeholders.
- Inputs and outputs are clearly named.
- Business rules and exceptions are linked to the business rules document.
- Requirements derived from processes are traceable to functional requirements.
