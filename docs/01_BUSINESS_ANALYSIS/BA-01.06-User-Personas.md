# BA-01.06 User Personas

## Persona Purpose

Personas represent common stakeholder needs and help ensure requirements, workflows, and user experiences are grounded in real usage patterns.

## Representative Personas

| Persona | Goals | Needs | Success Factors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | Understand progress, value, risk, and strategic alignment. | Dashboards, summaries, roadmap visibility, governance decisions. | Clear reporting and confidence that priorities are controlled. |
| Product owner | Translate business needs into prioritized requirements. | Process clarity, stakeholder feedback, acceptance criteria, backlog traceability. | Requirements are validated and implementation-ready. |
| Institution administrator | Configure organization, users, records, and operational data. | Reliable administration tools, bulk actions, audit history, validation. | Administrative tasks are accurate and efficient. |
| Academic leader | Manage academic structure, curriculum, assessment policy, and outcomes. | Academic setup workflows, reporting, curriculum and assessment visibility. | Academic operations are consistent and measurable. |
| Teacher | Manage classes, assessments, feedback, and learner progress. | Simple workflows, accurate student lists, grading support, notifications. | Teaching tasks are completed with less administrative friction. |
| Student | Access learning information, assessments, results, and announcements. | Secure access, mobile-friendly experience, clear status and feedback. | Information is easy to find and timely. |
| Guardian | Monitor relevant student information and communications. | Portal access, notifications, progress visibility, privacy controls. | Receives appropriate information without confusion. |
| Support operator | Resolve issues and maintain service quality. | Audit logs, support tools, known procedures, escalation paths. | Incidents are diagnosed and resolved quickly. |

## Persona Usage

- Link requirements to the personas that benefit from or are affected by them.
- Validate usability and communication needs with representative users.
- Revisit personas when stakeholder groups, access models, or product scope changes.
