# BA-01.07 Functional Requirements

## Requirement Format

Functional requirements should describe observable system behavior. Use clear identifiers, business rationale, affected personas, priority, acceptance criteria, and related business rules.

## Requirement Inventory

| ID | Capability | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR-001 | Identity and access | The platform shall allow authorized administrators to create, update, disable, and audit user accounts. | High |
| FR-002 | Identity and access | The platform shall enforce role-based access to modules, records, and administrative functions. | High |
| FR-003 | Organization | The platform shall maintain institution, unit, class, and group structures. | High |
| FR-004 | Academic structure | The platform shall define academic years, terms, levels, subjects, and schedules. | High |
| FR-005 | Student management | The platform shall maintain student profiles, enrollment status, guardian links, and lifecycle history. | High |
| FR-006 | Teacher management | The platform shall maintain teacher profiles, assignments, workload, and availability information. | Medium |
| FR-007 | Curriculum | The platform shall document curriculum structures, competencies, and learning mappings. | Medium |
| FR-008 | Assessment | The platform shall support assessment setup, score capture, grading rules, and progress review. | High |
| FR-009 | Reporting | The platform shall provide dashboards, reports, and exports based on approved data definitions. | High |
| FR-010 | Notifications | The platform shall deliver configurable notifications to selected stakeholder groups. | Medium |
| FR-011 | Document management | The platform shall support reusable templates and generated documents with approval history where required. | Medium |
| FR-012 | AI services | The platform shall provide AI-assisted features only where inputs, outputs, and review expectations are governed. | Low |

## Acceptance Criteria Guidance

Each requirement should define testable outcomes, expected user permissions, validation rules, error states, reporting or audit needs, and dependencies on other capabilities.
