# BA-01.09 Risk Analysis

## Risk Management Approach

Risks should be identified early, assigned an owner, assessed for likelihood and impact, and tracked with mitigation actions. This document focuses on business and delivery risks that affect DMDE planning and requirements.

## Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 | Stakeholder availability delays requirement validation. | Medium | High | Schedule recurring reviews and define delegated reviewers. |
| R-002 | Scope expands beyond agreed roadmap priorities. | Medium | High | Use change control and maintain clear in-scope and out-of-scope boundaries. |
| R-003 | Business rules differ across institutions or departments. | High | Medium | Document configurable rules and identify defaults, exceptions, and owners. |
| R-004 | Data definitions are inconsistent across modules. | Medium | High | Maintain glossary, data dictionary, and architecture review for shared data. |
| R-005 | Sensitive information is accidentally committed. | Medium | High | Follow security policy, use placeholders, and review assets before merge. |
| R-006 | Reporting expectations exceed available data quality. | Medium | Medium | Define source of truth, data quality rules, and reporting acceptance criteria. |
| R-007 | AI-assisted features create unclear accountability. | Medium | Medium | Require governance, human review, and documented input/output expectations. |
| R-008 | Documentation becomes stale after implementation changes. | Medium | Medium | Include documentation updates in delivery workflow and release readiness checks. |

## Risk Review Triggers

- Scope or roadmap changes.
- New stakeholder group or institution type added.
- New integration, AI capability, reporting model, or sensitive data flow introduced.
- Major architecture or operational decision approved.
