# BA-01.10 Business Rules

## Rule Documentation Approach

Business rules define constraints, calculations, decisions, and policy expectations that must be applied consistently. Rules should be owned, testable, and linked to relevant processes and requirements.

## Rule Categories

| Category | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access rules | Define who can view, create, update, approve, or delete records. | Only authorized administrators can change user roles. |
| Validation rules | Define required fields, allowed values, and data quality constraints. | Student records must include an active enrollment status before appearing in class lists. |
| Workflow rules | Define process steps, approvals, state changes, and exceptions. | Assessment results must be reviewed before publication when review is required by policy. |
| Calculation rules | Define formulas, aggregations, scoring, and reporting logic. | Progress summaries use approved assessment weights for the academic period. |
| Notification rules | Define recipients, triggers, templates, and delivery timing. | Guardians receive notifications only for information they are authorized to view. |
| Retention rules | Define record retention, archival, and deletion expectations. | Audit records are retained according to approved policy. |
| AI governance rules | Define when AI assistance is allowed and how outputs are reviewed. | AI-generated recommendations require human review before operational use. |

## Initial Business Rules

| ID | Rule | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-001 | Role assignments must follow approved identity and access governance. | Identity owner |
| BR-002 | Academic structures must be approved before schedules, assessments, or reports depend on them. | Academic owner |
| BR-003 | Student and teacher records must preserve lifecycle history for audit and reporting. | Business owner |
| BR-004 | Reports must identify their source data and approved definitions. | Reporting owner |
| BR-005 | Sensitive examples must use placeholders rather than real credentials or personal data. | Documentation maintainer |

## Maintenance Guidance

- Assign an owner to each rule before it is used for acceptance decisions.
- Link rules to processes, requirements, and affected domains.
- Record rule changes in the changelog when they materially affect behavior or reporting.
