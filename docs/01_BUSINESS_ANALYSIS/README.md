# Business Analysis

The business analysis section captures the business context, capabilities, organization model, domains, processes, personas, requirements, risks, and rules that shape DMDE delivery. It connects project vision to implementable product and architecture decisions.

## Documents

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `BA-01.01-Business-Overview.md` | Summarizes business context, value proposition, operating model, and outcomes. |
| `BA-01.02-Business-Capabilities.md` | Defines capability groups and their expected business value. |
| `BA-01.03-Organization-Structure.md` | Describes organizational actors, governance relationships, and decision paths. |
| `BA-01.04-Business-Domains.md` | Defines major business domains and ownership boundaries. |
| `BA-01.05-Business-Processes.md` | Documents core end-to-end business processes and workflow expectations. |
| `BA-01.06-User-Personas.md` | Identifies representative users, goals, needs, and success factors. |
| `BA-01.07-Functional-Requirements.md` | Captures functional requirements by capability area. |
| `BA-01.08-NonFunctional-Requirements.md` | Captures quality, security, compliance, performance, and operational requirements. |
| `BA-01.09-Risk-Analysis.md` | Identifies business, delivery, operational, security, and adoption risks. |
| `BA-01.10-Business-Rules.md` | Records policy rules, validation rules, workflow rules, and decision rules. |

## Maintenance Guidance

- Validate business analysis content with product owners and impacted stakeholders.
- Keep requirements traceable to objectives, processes, personas, and business rules.
- Update risk analysis when assumptions, scope, or dependencies change.
- Link architecture decisions back to business capabilities and non-functional requirements where possible.
