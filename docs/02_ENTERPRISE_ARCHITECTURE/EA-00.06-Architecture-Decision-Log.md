# EA-00.06 Architecture Decision Log

## Purpose

The architecture decision log summarizes material architecture decisions and links to detailed records in `ADR/` or `TDR/`.

## Decision Log

| ID | Decision | Status | Owner | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADR-0001 | Establish enterprise architecture documentation structure. | Proposed | Architecture owner | `ADR/` |
| TDR-0001 | Define technical record template and review workflow. | Proposed | Technical lead | `TDR/` |

## Decision Status Values

- `Proposed`: decision is drafted and awaiting review.
- `Accepted`: decision is approved for implementation or guidance.
- `Superseded`: decision has been replaced by a newer decision.
- `Deprecated`: decision should no longer be used for new work.

## Decision Criteria

Decisions should include context, options considered, rationale, consequences, owners, affected domains, and review date.
