# EA-00.12 AI Architecture

## AI Architecture Purpose

AI capabilities in DMDE should increase productivity and insight while preserving human accountability, privacy, security, and explainability.

## AI Use Cases

| Use Case | Governance Need |
| --- | --- |
| Content assistance | Review generated text before publication or operational use. |
| Summarization | Confirm source material, access rights, and output accuracy. |
| Recommendation | Define decision ownership and avoid fully automated high-impact outcomes. |
| Analytics assistance | Validate calculations, source data, and assumptions. |
| Support assistance | Prevent exposure of secrets, personal data, or unauthorized records. |

## Control Requirements

- Identify allowed inputs, restricted inputs, and output review expectations.
- Log AI-assisted actions where auditability is required.
- Evaluate quality, bias, safety, privacy, and operational risk before release.
- Provide human review for decisions that affect student, teacher, or institutional outcomes.
- Document prompts, models, data sources, and evaluation approach when material.
